ℹ️ About this job — in plain English

This building's car park sits on top of a concrete deck — like a giant flat roof you park cars on. The hidden waterproof layer under the surface has worn out, letting rainwater soak into the structure. We're stripping the deck back, fitting a brand-new waterproof "skin", and putting the surface back exactly as it was — so it looks the same, but is sealed and protected for decades.

How the waterproofing works: we bond down a CETCO Hydrofix 600 membrane — a tough, rubbery waterproof blanket laid in two overlapping layers so there's no gap for water anywhere — then a thin drainage mat carries surface water away to the drains, and the paving goes back on top.

The size of it: about 1,480 m² of deck (≈ 6 tennis courts), around 74,000 paving blocks lifted and re-laid by hand, and 3 brick planters removed — worked in small bays so as much of the car park stays usable as possible.
Step by step: 1) Paperwork & design · 2) Set up site (fencing, welfare, protection) · 3) Remove the 3 planters · 4) Lift the old paving & strip the worn-out waterproofing · 5) Lay the new waterproof skin · 6) Re-lay paving & repaint the bay lines · 7) Snagging with the client · 8) Clean up & handover.
